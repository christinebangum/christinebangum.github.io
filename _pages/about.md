**Welcome!**

I am a third-year PhD student in Economics at [BI Norwegian Business School](https://www.bi.edu/research/find-department/department-of-economics/). My main supervisor is Professor [Jon Fiva](https://www.jon.fiva.no/) and co-supervisor Professor [Benny Geys](https://www.bi.edu/about-bi/employees/department-of-economics/benny-geys/). My research interests include intra-party politics, political representation, voting behavior and pro-sociality.

I earned my M.A. in Political Science from the University of Oslo in January 2022. I have also worked for the [Norwegian National Election Study](https://www.samfunnsforskning.no/english/projects/aktive/stortingsvalgundersokelsene-2017-og-2021-eng.html). Before academia, I spent most of my time on environmental and climate politics.
