**Welcome!**

I am a fourth-year PhD student in Economics at [BI Norwegian Business School](https://www.bi.edu/research/find-department/department-of-economics/). My main supervisor is Professor [Jon Fiva](https://www.jon.fiva.no/) and co-supervisor Professor [Benny Geys](https://www.bi.edu/about-bi/employees/department-of-economics/benny-geys/). My research interests include charitable giving, pro-sociality, intra-party politics and electoral behavior.

Starting September 2026, I will join the [Institute for Social Research](https://www.samfunnsforskning.no/english/) as a Senior Research Fellow.

I earned my M.A. in Political Science from the University of Oslo in January 2022. Before academia, I spent most of my time on environmental and climate politics.
